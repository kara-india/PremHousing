## Rate-evidence proposal

- Draft starting points: 10 registered sale records / 12 months; 12 completed rental records / 12 months; 15 authorized active sale asks / 90 days; 15 authorized rent asks / 60 days. Ask/rent observations also need periodic availability refreshes. Each map sample must span multiple distinct properties/buildings; asking-price/rent map color proposals additionally call for several authorized contributors to reduce single-source bias.
- These are operational proposals, not statistical guarantees or NHB/RBI standards. NHB RESIDEX uses city-level product medians, transaction-count weighting, and a four-quarter moving average; RBI describes divergence between registered-transaction and advertised-price series. They inform the need for separation and segmentation, but neither supplies Mira Road or micro-area cutoffs. [NHB RESIDEX methodology](https://www.nhb.org.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/WHITE-PAPER-NHB-RESIDEX-METHODOLOGY.pdf) · [RBI HPI/web price comparison](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewBulletin.aspx?Id=19659).
- OSM licensing notes are based on [OSMF attribution guidance](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence/Attribution_Guidelines), [Produced Work guidance](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence/Community_Guidelines/Produced_Work_-_Guideline), and [OSMF licensing FAQ](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence_and_Legal_FAQ). Attribution should be visible, legible, and near the map; data/derivative database metadata should identify source/licence. Exact share-alike obligations depend on how combined datasets are structured and distributed.
