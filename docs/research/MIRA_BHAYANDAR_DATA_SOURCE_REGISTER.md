# Mira–Bhayandar data source register

**Research status:** Phase 0 desk research, 23 November 2026. This register is a source-eligibility assessment, not evidence that a dataset was downloaded, licensed directly, or verified for Mira–Bhayandar coverage. Publicly viewable information is not automatically licensed for bulk reuse.

## Decision summary

- Start with PREM-submitted property/listing data where the submitter confirms authority and publication rights, and Overture Places after checking actual local coverage and preserving source-level license metadata. OSM extracts are a viable alternative with ODbL obligations; do not use the public OSM tile or Nominatim services as a bulk data feed.
- Use official sites as linked verification references until PREM has written reuse terms, a documented API, or an applicable dataset-level open license. This applies especially to MBMC directories/planning maps, MahaRERA, IGR and MMRDA material.
- No authoritative, openly licensed neighborhood boundary dataset for Mira Road East/West or Bhayandar East/West was verified. Do not invent polygons from postal labels, ward sketches, road buffers, or user search boxes. Keep locality names/aliases unbounded until MBMC/other authority provides suitable GIS data or PREM explicitly labels an approximate, user-drawn service area.
- No verified locality-scale flood, air-quality, transit travel-time, school-quality, or market-price feed is approved by this research. Do not turn regional planning documents, project announcements, portal asking prices, or aggregate census figures into claims about a specific locality.

## Pending source requests / gates

1. Ask MBMC Town Planning/GIS for the authoritative locality/ward boundary vector layer, CRS, publication date, boundary definitions, change history, and written commercial reuse/attribution terms. Ask the relevant MBMC department for a machine-readable, permissioned public amenities feed.
2. Ask MMRDA for transport station/alignment GIS and explicit rights to redistribute derivative locations; confirm each asset’s current operational status and dates.
3. For MahaRERA and IGR, do not build extraction jobs unless an official documented API/export right is located and terms/privacy review approve the specific fields and retention.
4. Run a non-publishing coverage audit for Overture Places and a licensed OSM extract over a defined, approved study area. Record release IDs, sources, missing/duplicate rates and field checks before selecting a production POI source.
5. Identify authoritative, reusable local flood/drainage, air-quality, water/noise, and public transport service datasets. Until then, display no computed local risk or travel-time score.
6. For every data.gov.in resource, save the resource-level license metadata and attribution at the time of ingestion. The portal-wide license does not override third-party exclusions.

## Unresolved decisions

- Which locality names are product entities, and what authority will define their shapes? Mira Road East/West, Bhayandar East/West, Kashimira, and similar labels may not be official bounded geographies.
- Is PREM willing to publish OSM-derived database content under ODbL when required, or should OSM-derived data be kept in a separately managed source/database with provider/legal review?
- What data-use and display rights will be collected from staff, brokers, owners, and community contributors, including exact-coordinate consent and media rights?
- What is the public map precision policy for listings, private residences, user reports, and small-cell observations?
- Which data sources will be used for factual claims and any future market estimates? What minimum sample size, time window, property-area basis, confidence interval, and suppression threshold must be met?
- Who owns scheduled source freshness checks, corrections/takedown, license review, and data deletion when a source right expires?
