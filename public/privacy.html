Privacy notice

When you contact Prem Housing, you choose to share the details you provide so the team can respond to your property enquiry. Please do not submit identity documents, financial account information or other sensitive documents through this website.

The enquiry form opens WhatsApp with a draft message. You must press Send in WhatsApp for the information to be shared. WhatsApp processes messages under its own terms and privacy policy.

For questions about a submitted enquiry or to request correction of your contact details, contact Prem Housing using the telephone number displayed on the website.

This notice is an initial operational notice and should be reviewed and completed with the business's legal contact details and applicable privacy requirements before paid advertising is launched.
